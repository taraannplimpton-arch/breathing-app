How to use Breathe

On the home screen, choose a pattern card: Calm Down, Energy, HRV, Focus, Sleep, or Avoid the Urge.

Choose 1, 2, 5, or 10 minutes, then Start. The usual length is 5 minutes. Energy is always one minute. Avoid the Urge is about three minutes and ignores the minute picker. It also skips body setup.

During a session you can Pause, Ease (shorter counts from the next breath), or End. When the time finishes, the screen waits with “What's the smallest next move?” Tap to go home.

Body setup, soft audio, haptics, and humming are toggles on the home screen. Humming applies to Calm Down and Sleep only.

FAQ

No sound when the iPhone is in silent mode

The iPhone silent switch mutes Soft audio. Turn silent mode off if you want to hear the tone. Pause and End also mute the tone.

Haptics on iPhone

Haptics are unavailable in iPhone web browsers, including a site saved to the Home Screen. The toggle is turned off and labeled unavailable there. Soft audio is the cue on iPhone.

How to reset settings

Open About from the info icon on the home screen, then choose Reset settings. Confirm. Only this app’s saved preferences on this device are cleared, and the defaults come back. Your other apps are left alone.
