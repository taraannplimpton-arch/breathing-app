How to use Breathwork

On the home screen, choose a pattern card: Calm Down, Energy, HRV, Focus, Sleep, or Think First.

Choose 1, 2, 5, or 10 minutes, then Start. The usual length is 5 minutes. Energy is always one minute. Think First is about three minutes and ignores the minute picker. It also skips body setup.

During a session you can Pause, Ease (shorter counts from the next breath), or End. When the time finishes, the screen waits with “What's the smallest next move?” Tap to go home.

Body setup, soft audio, haptics, and humming are switches in Settings. Humming applies to Calm Down and Sleep only.

FAQ

No sound when the iPhone is in silent mode

The iPhone silent switch mutes Soft audio. Turn silent mode off if you want to hear the tone. Pause and End also mute the tone.

Haptics on iPhone

Haptics are hidden in iPhone web browsers, including a site saved to the Home Screen. Those browsers do not vibrate. The switch appears in a wrapper with native haptics, or in a browser that can vibrate. Soft audio is the cue on iPhone.

Daily reminder

The daily reminder is off. It appears only inside a wrapper that can schedule a notification on the device itself, with no push service. Turning it off does not cancel a reminder already scheduled. The next one is set the next time you open the app.

How to reset settings

Open About from Information on the home screen, then choose Reset settings. Confirm. Only this app’s saved preferences on this device are cleared, and the defaults come back. Your other apps are left alone.
