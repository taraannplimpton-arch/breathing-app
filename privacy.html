Privacy

Breathe does not collect data. There is no account, no analytics, and no tracking.

Preferences, such as session length and the audio, haptics, timer, body setup, and hum toggles, stay on this device in localStorage. They are not sent to a server. There are no third parties.

The app does not ask for your name, email, contacts, health records, or location. A breathing session is not saved.

Questions: support@example.com
