About

Practical Breathing is a quiet breathing practice you run on your own device. Six cards: Calm Down, Energy, HRV, Focus, Sleep, and Think First. Nothing you do here is uploaded.

How to use it

Pick a card. Choose 1, 2, 5, or 10 minutes, then Start. Energy is always one minute. Think First is about three minutes and does not use that picker. Pause, Ease, or End whenever you want. Body setup, when it is on, shows a few words before a timed session. Soft audio and haptics stay off until you turn them on in Settings. Humming is for Calm Down and Sleep.

Think First: That airport candy, the reply-all, the cart checkout. Breathe first, then decide.

Safety

Stop if you feel faint, dizzy, panicky, or have chest pain. Consult a doctor first if you are pregnant or you have a heart, lung, seizure, or other significant condition. This app is educational. It is not medical advice, and it is not for emergencies.
