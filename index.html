About

Practical Breathing is a breathing practice you run on your own device. Six cards for six moments: Calm Down, Energy, HRV, Focus, Sleep, and Think First. Nothing you do here is uploaded.

How to use it

Pick a card

  • Calm Down: headed into a presentation, or after a tense conversation.
  • Energy (always 1 minute): during a meeting, before a race or a mahjong game.
  • Focus: before deep work, studying, or a tough email.
  • HRV: slow, even breathing, about 6 breaths a minute. Use it anytime for longevity and heart health.
  • Sleep: right at bedtime, or if you wake in the middle of the night.
  • Think First (always 3 minutes): use it to avoid a choice you'll regret, like Twizzlers at the airport, a glass of wine instead of water, or scrolling.

Pick a time

Except for Energy and Think First, choose 1, 2, 5, or 10 minutes, then Start. Each card remembers your last time and settings.

Check settings

In Settings you can always show the timer while breathing, use soft audio (helpful with your eyes closed), show a short body setup before each session, and hum on the exhale for Calm Down and Sleep.

If you use soft audio, make sure your iPhone isn't on silent.

Body setup

Place your feet comfortably. Stack your body without bracing: tall or long in a way that is easy. Let your hands rest somewhere quiet. Relax your jaw, with your tongue resting and a bit of space between your teeth. Lower your gaze or close your eyes. Then take one easy exhale before starting.

During a session

Tap Pause to stop for a moment, or End to finish early. Tap Ease to shorten each count by about a second, starting with the next breath. You can tap it up to twice.

Safety

Stop if you feel faint, dizzy, panicky, or have chest pain. Consult a doctor first if you are pregnant or you have a heart, lung, seizure, or other significant condition. This app is educational. It is not medical advice, and it is not for emergencies.
